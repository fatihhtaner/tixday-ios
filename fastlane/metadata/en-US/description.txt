Every date you're waiting for deserves a ticket.

Tixday turns your countdowns into beautiful tickets: a boarding pass for your trip, a concert ticket for the show, an invitation for the wedding, a party pass for the birthday. Each one comes with its own vintage poster art and counts down on your Home Screen and Lock Screen.

TICKETS FOR EVERY OCCASION
• Trip, concert, exam, wedding, birthday and holiday tickets, each with its own design
• Fill in the details: route, seat, venue, time
• Birthdays and anniversaries repeat every year
• See how much of the wait is already behind you
• When the day has passed, your ticket gets its "Admitted" stamp

WIDGETS
• Small and medium Home Screen widgets
• Pick which ticket each widget shows, or always follow the next one
• Lock Screen widgets with Tixday Pro

REMINDERS
• A month, a week and a day before, and on the day itself, at the time you choose

YOUR TICKETS, SAFE
• Backed up to your private iCloud, so they come with you to a new iPhone
• No sign-up, no ads

TIXDAY PRO
• Unlimited tickets (free includes 3)
• Your own photos on tickets and widgets
• Lock Screen widgets
• New poster packs as they arrive

Pro is available as a yearly subscription with a free trial, or a one-time lifetime purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the period; manage them in your Apple ID settings.
