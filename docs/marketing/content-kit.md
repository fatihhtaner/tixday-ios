## 02 Reaction (20 s, split screen; use Stitch/Duet on public videos)

Top: a real comment or video about countdown apps (credit the source).
- Comment to look for: "Every countdown app wants a subscription."
- Bottom, face to camera: "I see this every day. So I made the basics free: three tickets, Home Screen widgets, reminders, no account."
- Then a screen recording of the home screen and a widget.
- CTA: "See for yourself, link in bio."
- Türkçe: "Bunu her gün görüyorum. O yüzden temelini ücretsiz yaptım: üç bilet, ana ekran widget'ları, hatırlatmalar, hesap yok. Kendin gör, link bio'da."

Only react to public content, never mock the person, and keep the claim exact (the free plan is 3 tickets).

## 07 Skits (20–35 s, one place, one phone)

**"How many days?"**
1. Two friends on a couch: "How many days until our trip?"
2. The other counts on their fingers, gives up, opens the calendar app.
3. The first one just shows their Home Screen: the boarding-pass widget, "83 DAYS".
4. "…okay, I'm downloading that." Caption: Tixday · link in bio.

**"The birthday"**
1. A phone buzzes in the morning: the reminder notification, "Tomorrow's the day!"
2. Panic face, then a calm smile.
3. Cut to a cake the next day; the widget on the phone shows "Every year".
4. Caption: Set it once. It comes back every year.

## 10 Micro influencers (5K–50K followers)

Niches that match:
- study / exam planners: the exam ticket
- travel planning: the boarding pass
- wedding planning: the invitation
- Home Screen aesthetic / widget setups: the core audience
- couples: birthdays and anniversaries

**What to give:**
- Pro for free: an App Store offer code for Yearly. Create it in App Store Connect → Subscriptions → Offer Codes.
- An optional fixed fee per video, or per install.
- A tracking link: an App Store campaign link, `https://apps.apple.com/app/apple-store/id6820981179?pt=<provider token>&ct=<creator>&mt=8`. The provider token is in App Store Connect → Analytics → Sources. Installs then show up per creator.

**DM (EN):** Hi [name], I love your [niche] videos. I'm Fatih, I made Tixday: every date becomes a vintage ticket on your Home Screen. I'd love to do 3 videos with you: Pro free for you, [fee] per video, and a bonus per install. I can send a short brief with hook ideas. Interested?

**DM (TR):** Merhaba [isim], [niş] içeriklerini çok seviyorum. Ben Fatih, Tixday'i yaptım: her tarih ana ekranda vintage bir bilete dönüşüyor. Seninle 3 videoluk bir iş birliği yapmak isterim: Pro senin için ücretsiz, video başına [ücret] ve kurulum başına bonus. Kısa bir brif ve hook önerileri gönderebilirim. İlgilenir misin?

**Brief (one page):**
- **Goal:** show your real countdown (trip, exam, wedding…) as a widget.
- **Hook ideas:** "My Home Screen is a ticket wallet now"; "How many days until [event]? Look"; "Stop counting days in Notes".
- **Must say:** the name Tixday; free to start; link in bio / your code.
- **Must not say:** anything the app doesn't do; ratings or numbers we don't have.
- **Legal:** the platform's paid partnership label plus #ad / #reklam.
- **Delivery:** 7 days, raw file for reuse in ads, 90-day ad usage right.

After 2 weeks, keep the best 5 creators and drop the rest.

## 13 Clipping

## 15 Turning winners into ads (after 10 organic posts)

Pick the top 3 by:
- completion rate
- saves and shares
- "link?" comments
- profile clicks

Boost them as TikTok Spark Ads or Meta partnership ads. Raise the budget 20–30% at a time. Pin the best real comment under the post.

Checklist before boosting:
- music from the platform library only
- the AI label on voiced tutorials (synthetic macOS voice)
- the claim matches the App Store page
